# Test Reporting

## Purpose

The Novus CI pipeline produces standardized reports to assist debugging, quality monitoring, and regression analysis.

Reports are generated automatically during Continuous Integration.

---

# Coverage

Coverage reports are generated by packages supporting coverage collection.

Typical outputs include:

```text
coverage/
├── index.html
├── lcov.info
├── coverage-final.json
└── ...
```

Coverage reports are uploaded as CI artifacts when available.

---

# Test Results

Framework-specific test outputs are collected under:

```text
test-results/
```

These reports assist debugging failed CI executions.

---

# JUnit Reports

JUnit-compatible XML reports should be generated when supported.

Standard location:

```text
junit/
└── junit.xml
```

These reports allow future integration with reporting dashboards.

---

# Playwright Reports

Browser automation reports are generated by the Playwright Pipeline.

Standard location:

```text
playwright-report/
```

---

# Artifact Policy

The CI pipeline uploads diagnostic artifacts even when tests fail.

Typical artifacts include:

- Coverage reports
- Test results
- Playwright reports
- Build logs

Artifacts are retained for a limited period according to repository policy.

---

# Generated Files

The following directories are generated automatically and should not be committed:

```text
coverage/
playwright-report/
test-results/
artifacts/
```

These directories are ignored through `.gitignore`.

---

# Reporting Philosophy

Every CI execution should leave behind enough information to diagnose failures without reproducing them locally.

Reports are considered first-class engineering artifacts and form an essential part of the platform's quality assurance process.
